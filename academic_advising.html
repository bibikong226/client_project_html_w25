Academic Advising

Academic Resources Overview

UMSI supports student success through academic advising, library resources, tutoring, and the Writing Center. These services help you succeed in your studies, master complex material, and communicate effectively in writing.

Academic Advising

Advising is a collaborative process where students build academic plans with guidance from their advisor. Advisors help with policies, planning, and aligning your academic journey with your personal and professional goals.

Important Dates

  • January 28: Add/Drop Deadline
  • March 1–9: Spring Break
  • March 19: Backpacking begins
  • March 26–28: Registration for graduate students

How Advisors Support You

  • Creating a personalized academic plan
  • Connecting with campus support resources
  • Clarifying policies and procedures
  • Navigating challenges in your academic journey
  • Discussing course concerns

  • Writing Center: One-on-one help with course papers, resumes, and more.
